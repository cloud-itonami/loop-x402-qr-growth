# Operating contract

## Loop

1. Observe the capability document and release; preserve status, timestamp, and digest.
2. Research one market hypothesis using current official regulator/provider sources. Cover stablecoin acceptance, operating entity, refunds, receipts, privacy, and hardware integration.
3. Identify up to five merchants or terminal vendors using published business information. Record relevance, provenance, and contact authorization; do not contact people without explicit authorization.
4. Prepare one localized pilot proposal stating actual rail, wallet requirements, operator responsibilities, support, and hardware gaps.
5. Reconcile pilot evidence. A signature is not settlement; an issuance command is not a printed ticket.
6. Save a dated report with the next bounded action. Missing evidence stays a named blocker.

## Country admission

hypothesis → researched → operator-reviewed → pilot-authorized → live-qualified. No automatic admission to the last two stages. EEA and LATAM are research buckets and must resolve to individual jurisdictions. Require actual contracting entity, applicable legal review, supported wallets and network, settlement arrangement, pricing/tax/receipts, privacy, dispute/refund handling, support language, terminal SDK and physical tests. Record reviewer, scope, and date; never invent the entity.

## Pilot acceptance

Prove authorized payment (testnet/mainnet separately), transaction, correct order/merchant/amount, one physical ticket, durable receipt, daily reconciliation. Exercise duplicates, expiry, wrong network/amount/recipient, timeout, disconnect, paper-out, and crash after print before acknowledgment. Non-idempotent printers cannot promise exactly-once physical delivery.

## Metrics and authority

Use denominators and windows: qualified/contacted merchants, activated/authorized pilots, settled/approved attempts, physically-issued/paid orders, duplicates, unresolved paid orders, latency p50/p95, external merchant GMV. Exclude internal, simulated, testnet, refunded, and unconfirmed transactions. Unmeasured is not zero.

Destination policy controls tools, accounts, spending, messages, writes, schedules, and deploys. The profile grants none. No autonomous outreach or paid acquisition is enabled by this repository.
